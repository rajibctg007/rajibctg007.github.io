About Me

An experienced apparel quality professional focused on building reliable systems, improving product quality, developing people, and strengthening factory performance.

My Professional Approach

With 16+ years of experience across garment manufacturing and buying-house quality functions, I have developed practical knowledge of buyer requirements, production quality, factory audits, shipment readiness, and quality team leadership.

I believe quality should be built into every stage of production rather than inspected into the product at the end. My approach emphasizes prevention, root-cause analysis, accountability, teamwork, and continuous improvement.

My long-term ambition is to contribute to senior quality and technical leadership while sharing practical knowledge through apparel quality training and consultancy.
