About Me

I am an apparel quality professional with 16+ years of industry experience spanning factory quality operations, buying-house quality management, buyer requirements, audit readiness, and team leadership. My focus is on practical problem-solving, preventive quality systems, continuous improvement, and building accountability across production and quality teams.

I believe quality should be built into every stage of production, not inspected into the product at the end. My professional goal is to help organizations achieve consistent quality, stronger factory performance, and sustainable improvement.
